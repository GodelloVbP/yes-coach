Yes Coach! connector for Claude

Connect your own Claude to your Yes Coach! account. Claude can read your plan, your logged sessions and your notes, change your plan, and log what you eat, so you can ask "what is my training plan today?", "make Thursday lighter on the shoulder" or say "2 slices of bread with egg, cheese and mustard" and see it in the app straight away.

What you need

  • A Yes Coach! account (create one, free).
  • A Claude account. Free, Pro and Max add the connector themselves (Free allows one custom connector). On Team and Enterprise, an Owner adds it once for the organization and each member then connects with their own Yes Coach! login.

Connect

  1. In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  2. Name: Yes Coach!. Server URL: https://qazvzhhoalkyyasakphs.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp. If asked how Claude identifies itself, choose Register automatically.
  3. Click Add, then Connect. A Yes Coach! page opens. Sign in with your Yes Coach! email and password.
  4. Read what the app may do, then click Allow.
  5. Ask Claude: "What is in my Yes Coach! plan?"

In Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http yes-coach https://qazvzhhoalkyyasakphs.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp

What Claude can do

ToolWhat it does
Get overviewYour profile, weekly schedule, training days, last sessions, notes waiting.
Get training planEvery training day with exercises, sets, reps, starting load, rest and notes.
List exercisesYour exercises and how each is counted (kg, machine pin, bodyweight, assisted, minutes).
Get logged sessionsYour logged sets per session, newest first, optionally between two dates.
Add sets to the logAdds done sets of one exercise to a day, for when something was not logged or was logged under the wrong exercise. Sets cannot be edited afterwards.
Remove sets from the logTakes sets out of a day's log, by set or all sets of one exercise. They leave your history and Progress.
Get exercise historyHow one exercise went over time: best working set per session.
Get notesNotes you wrote during sessions.
Create exerciseAdds an exercise that is not in your list yet.
Save training dayCreates a training day or replaces the exercise list of an existing one.
Set weekly scheduleChooses which days are in the week and when. The app keeps your previous schedule so you can undo it.
Answer noteWrites a reply under one of your notes.
Search foodsFinds foods in the Dutch (NEVO) and international (USDA) tables the app uses, with nutrients per 100 g and the portions each comes in, such as a slice or a piece.
Get food dayWhat you ate on one day, the day's totals and your daily targets. Entries you noted with "I ate something" show up as still to fill in, so you can ask Claude what you forgot.
Log mealsSaves meals to your food log. Claude picks the foods and amounts; the app reads calories, protein, carbs and fat from its own tables, so Claude does not make the numbers up. A meal with several foods is one entry with its items inside. Foods you log are remembered, so the next "kaas" finds your cheese first.
Get my foodsThe foods you logged before, most used first, with the words you use for them and your own portion sizes, plus the meals you saved.
Save custom foodSaves a food that is not in the tables, such as something home-made or a branded product, with the calories, protein, carbs and fat per 100 g or 100 ml from its package. Claude asks you for those numbers; it does not guess them. The food then comes first in searches and can be logged by grams or portion. You can also create one yourself in the app, under Food.
Save my foodRemembers which food you mean by a word, and your own portion sizes (your slice of bread is 40 g, for example). Claude uses them the next time.
Save mealSaves a meal you eat often, from what you tell Claude or from something you already logged, so you can log it again with one tap or by name.
Remove saved food or mealForgets a remembered food or removes a saved meal, when you ask. Entries already in your food log stay.
Remove food entryRemoves one entry from your food log, when you ask or when Claude logged it by mistake.

Claude asks you before it changes anything.

What it can not do

  • Change or delete anything you logged in a session.
  • Delete your plan, exercises or account, or touch your feedback tickets. Food entries are the one thing it can remove, one at a time. The database refuses these for connected apps, whatever the app tries.

Disconnect

In Yes Coach!: Settings, Connected AI apps, Disconnect. The app loses access straight away. You can also remove the connector in Claude.

Privacy

What Claude reads is sent to Claude and handled under Anthropic's terms. Yes Coach! stores nothing extra for the connector. See the privacy page.
